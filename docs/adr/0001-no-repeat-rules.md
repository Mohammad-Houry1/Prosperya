# No pinned Hero, and the Hub at most once per page

The Home Hero used to be a pinned story about three screens long in which one Hub graphic stayed on screen while text swapped. The Hub also recurred across about five surfaces, twice on Home. The owner found this repetitive, and none of the 14 sites sampled in `docs/research/2026-10-02-hero-and-repetition.md` pins its home hero. So every Hero now fits one screen and never pins, and a page shows the Hub at most once. As a result, the Home "Built around NetSuite" section deliberately uses a non-Hub visual even though "NetSuite at the core" reads naturally as a Hub. Any other Pinned story sits below the Hero, one per page, at about 2 to 2.5 screens.

## Considered Options

- Keep the pinned Home story but shorten it to about two screens: rejected, because it would still be the only top-of-page pin in the sample, and the graphic would still hold while the text changed.
- Allow the Hub wherever a section is about integration: rejected, because the motif stops being a signature once it appears on every page.
